## ARTICLE 8: INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY
### 8.1 Vendor Indemnification
Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer against any third-party claims alleging that the Cloud Services infringe any valid copyright, patent, or trade secret.

### 8.3 Limitation of Liability
IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE CAPPED AT TWELVE (12) MONTHS OF FEES PAID PRECEDING THE CLAIM OR $1,500,000, WHICHEVER IS GREATER.

## ARTICLE 11: SECURITY AND AUDITS
### 11.4 Security Certifications
Vendor warrants that it shall maintain annual SOC-2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications throughout the term of this Agreement. Upon written request, Vendor shall provide Customer with a copy of its current SOC-2 Type II audit report within thirty (30) days.

## ARTICLE 14: TERM AND TERMINATION
### 14.2 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon providing at least sixty (60) days prior written notice to the other party.
